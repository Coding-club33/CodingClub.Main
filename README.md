# Coding Club — JDT Islam College of Arts and Science

**[codingclubjdt.in](https://codingclubjdt.in)** is the official website of the Coding Club at JDT Islam College of Arts and Science, Kozhikode, Kerala.

---

## What is this?

CodingClubJDT.in was our initiative to create a community that helps juniors and freshers get a head start in technology. We organized various workshops, hackathons, and IT fests that provided newcomers with valuable opportunities to learn coding and explore different areas of computer science and technology. Through these activities, we aimed to build a strong foundation for students and foster a culture of innovation and collaboration within the department.

The website serves as the public face of the club — showcasing who we are, what we've done, and who's part of the community.

---

## What's on the site

- **Home** — Club intro, what we focus on, and a carousel of past programs
- **Programs** — A detailed gallery of every session and event we've conducted, including orientation classes, Photoshop workshops, PPT competitions, and HTML/CSS sessions
- **Members** — The current executive team and past executives who built the club
- **Login** — A member portal (in progress)

---

## Activities we've run

| Activity | Description |
|---|---|
| Orientation Class | Introductory session led by the Head of Department, covering the club's vision and goals |
| HTML & CSS Workshop | Hands-on web development class for club members, led by final-year BCA students |
| Photoshop Class | Graphic design basics covering image editing, layering, and colour correction |
| PPT Idea Presentation | Competition where students pitched tech solutions and innovative ideas |

---

## The team behind it

The website was designed and built entirely by club members — students from the BCA & CS department at JDT Islam College. The 2024–25 executive team led by Muhammed Anshik N built the first version of the site as part of the club's effort to establish an online presence.
